Hi, I’m Heba

I have ADHD, so I learn a million things at once and my notes pile up on top of each other.

In the middle of all that, I’m learning my religion properly, slowly, one step at a time. So I thought: why not bring you along and learn together?

If long lectures and endless note-taking are hard for you too, I’ve got you. Each hadith gets a short, simple explanation that keeps the important meanings, plus a PDF with the essentials.

I’m learning with you, and I always share my sources. If I get something wrong, please correct me gently 🤍
