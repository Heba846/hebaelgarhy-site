Is one glass a day healthy?

1,400 years ago the Quran said “their sin is greater than their benefit.” In 2018, a study of 195 countries found the safest amount is zero.

Sources

  • Quran 2:219
  • “Whatever intoxicates in large amounts, a little of it is forbidden”: Abu Dawud 3681, Tirmidhi 1865 (hasan)
  • GBD 2016 Alcohol Collaborators. The Lancet 2018;392:1015–35
  • Anderson et al. (WHO Europe). Lancet Public Health 2023;8(1):e6–e7
