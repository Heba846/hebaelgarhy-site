The Stages of Creation and Divine Decree

Creation, qadar, and a good ending

A simple explanation of the hadith of Ibn Mas'ud about the creation of the human being, the fourth hadith in Imam an-Nawawi's Forty Hadith. Learn the stages of creation in the womb, when the soul is breathed in according to the majority of scholars, the writing of provision, lifespan and deeds, belief in qadar (divine decree), and why deeds are judged by how they end.

The hadith

عَنْ أَبِي عَبْدِ الرَّحْمَنِ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ مَسْعُودٍ رَضِيَ اللهُ عَنْهُ قَالَ: حَدَّثَنَا رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه و سلم -وَهُوَ الصَّادِقُ الْمَصْدُوقُ-: "إنَّ أَحَدَكُمْ يُجْمَعُ خَلْقُهُ فِي بَطْنِ أُمِّهِ أَرْبَعِينَ يَوْمًا نُطْفَةً، ثُمَّ يَكُونُ عَلَقَةً مِثْلَ ذَلِكَ، ثُمَّ يَكُونُ مُضْغَةً مِثْلَ ذَلِكَ، ثُمَّ يُرْسَلُ إلَيْهِ الْمَلَكُ فَيَنْفُخُ فِيهِ الرُّوحَ، وَيُؤْمَرُ بِأَرْبَعِ كَلِمَاتٍ: بِكَتْبِ رِزْقِهِ، وَأَجَلِهِ، وَعَمَلِهِ، وَشَقِيٍّ أَمْ سَعِيدٍ؛ فَوَاَللَّهِ الَّذِي لَا إلَهَ غَيْرُهُ إنَّ أَحَدَكُمْ لَيَعْمَلُ بِعَمَلِ أَهْلِ الْجَنَّةِ حَتَّى مَا يَكُونُ بَيْنَهُ وَبَيْنَهَا إلَّا ذِرَاعٌ فَيَسْبِقُ عَلَيْهِ الْكِتَابُ فَيَعْمَلُ بِعَمَلِ أَهْلِ النَّارِ فَيَدْخُلُهَا. وَإِنَّ أَحَدَكُمْ لَيَعْمَلُ بِعَمَلِ أَهْلِ النَّارِ حَتَّى مَا يَكُونُ بَيْنَهُ وَبَيْنَهَا إلَّا ذِرَاعٌ فَيَسْبِقُ عَلَيْهِ الْكِتَابُ فَيَعْمَلُ بِعَمَلِ أَهْلِ الْجَنَّةِ فَيَدْخُلُهَا". .

The Messenger of Allah (ﷺ), and he is the truthful, the believed, narrated to us, “Verily the creation of each one of you is brought together in his mother’s womb for forty days in the form of a nutfah (a drop), then he becomes an alaqah (clot of blood) for a like period, then a mudghah (morsel of flesh) for a like period, then there is sent to him the angel who blows his soul into him and who is commanded with four matters: to write down his rizq (sustenance), his life span, his actions, and whether he will be happy or unhappy (i.e., whether or not he will enter Paradise). By the One, other than Whom there is no deity, verily one of you performs the actions of the people of Paradise until there is but an arms length between him and it, and that which has been written overtakes him, and so he acts with the actions of the people of the Hellfire and thus enters it; and verily one of you performs the actions of the people of the Hellfire, until there is but an arms length between him and it, and that which has been written overtakes him and so he acts with the actions of the people of Paradise and thus he enters it.”

Reported by al-Bukhari and Muslim — an-Nawawi's Forty Hadith, no. 4

The hadith & its narrator

Assalamu alaikum, I'm Heba. If this is your first time here: I'm summarizing Imam an-Nawawi's Forty Hadith, one hadith at a time, using mind maps and simple explanations, for anyone who doesn't have time for long lessons. And remember: what cannot be done completely should not be left completely. So let's begin: hadith number four.

Imagine a page where your provision, your lifespan, your work, and your final destiny were all written down, while you were still in your mother's womb. If that's true, why should we work hard at all? That's exactly what we'll understand today.

This hadith is narrated by Abdullah ibn Mas'ud, may Allah be pleased with him. And remember the order: the first two hadiths are from Umar, the third is from Abdullah ibn Umar, and the fourth is from Abdullah ibn Mas'ud. It's reported by al-Bukhari and Muslim.

عَنْ أَبِي عَبْدِ الرَّحْمَنِ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ مَسْعُودٍ رَضِيَ اللهُ عَنْهُ قَالَ: حَدَّثَنَا رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه و سلم -وَهُوَ الصَّادِقُ الْمَصْدُوقُ-:

From Abu Abd ar-Rahman, Abdullah ibn Mas'ud, may Allah be pleased with him, who said: The Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, the truthful one, the one who is believed, told us:

Why did Ibn Mas'ud say: the truthful one, the one who is believed? Because what comes next, about the baby in its mother's womb, was something from the unseen that no one knew at the time. So he's telling us: believe it, this is revelation from Allah.

إنَّ أَحَدَكُمْ يُجْمَعُ خَلْقُهُ فِي بَطْنِ أُمِّهِ أَرْبَعِينَ يَوْمًا نُطْفَةً، ثُمَّ يَكُونُ عَلَقَةً مِثْلَ ذَلِكَ، ثُمَّ يَكُونُ مُضْغَةً مِثْلَ ذَلِكَ، ثُمَّ يُرْسَلُ إلَيْهِ الْمَلَكُ فَيَنْفُخُ فِيهِ الرُّوحَ،

The creation of each one of you is gathered in his mother's womb for forty days as a drop. Then he becomes a clinging clot for a similar period. Then a lump of flesh for a similar period. Then the angel is sent to him, and he breathes the soul into him.

Drop, clot, lump of flesh

Let's understand the words. Nutfah is the drop, the man's fluid. Alaqah is a clot of blood that clings to the wall of the womb, which is why it's called alaqah, the clinging thing. And mudghah is a small piece of flesh, as if it had been chewed.

40 days or 120?

And here's the hardest question in the hadith: when is the soul breathed in? The apparent meaning of the narration is that each stage is forty days, so the soul is breathed in after a hundred and twenty days. But some scholars understood that all of this happens within the first forty days, and that the word “then” here is about the order of speech.

Those who hold the second view use the hadith of Hudhayfah ibn Asid in Sahih Muslim: after forty-two nights, Allah sends an angel who shapes the baby, and creates its hearing, its sight, its skin, its flesh and its bones. So after only about forty days, there are already bones and features.

Also, another narration of Ibn Mas'ud's same hadith, in Muslim, says: “in that, the same as that”, meaning within the same period. And reality agrees: doctors see the baby's features at around seven weeks, and if it comes out after four months, it's a complete human being.

The majority of scholars hold that the soul is breathed in after a hundred and twenty days, which is the plain meaning of this hadith. Some scholars held other views, and the difference isn't just theory: it leads to very important rulings.

Effects on rulings

The first effect is on abortion. Before the soul is breathed in, scholars allow it when there's a strong reason, like a real risk to the mother's health, and it may even be required if her life is in danger. Doing it out of fear of poverty is forbidden. Allah says, meaning: Do not kill your children for fear of poverty. And without a real need, most scholars do not allow it.

After the soul is breathed in, at a hundred and twenty days, abortion is forbidden by consensus of the scholars, except for a compelling medical reason, like the mother's life being in danger. Every case has its own circumstances, so ask a mufti, a scholar qualified to give religious rulings.

The second effect: bleeding after a miscarriage. If it happens before forty days, it's menstrual blood, called hayd, which lasts fifteen days at most. If it's after a hundred and twenty days, it's postnatal bleeding, called nifas, which lasts forty days at most. And in between: if a human form is visible, it's nifas; if not, it's hayd.

The third effect: the miscarried baby itself. If a human form is visible, it's recommended to wash it, shroud it and pray over it, and burying it is obligatory. If not, it's wrapped in a cloth and buried.

The four matters

وَيُؤْمَرُ بِأَرْبَعِ كَلِمَاتٍ: بِكَتْبِ رِزْقِهِ، وَأَجَلِهِ، وَعَمَلِهِ، وَشَقِيٍّ أَمْ سَعِيدٍ؛

And he is commanded to write four things: his provision, his lifespan, his deeds, and whether he will be wretched or happy.

And remember the four levels of qadar, divine decree, from the hadith of Jibril: Allah knows everything, He wrote it all down, nothing happens except by His will, and He is the One who created our actions, while we are responsible for them. This hadith focuses on the level of writing.

Here's an example from the lesson: Allah is the One who created the child, but the father and mother are the ones responsible for him. It's the same with your deeds: Allah created them, but you are the cause of them, and you're held accountable based on them.

What the writing does for us

So what does this writing do for us? First, knowing your provision is written gives you peace of mind. As Hatim al-Asamm said: I knew that no one else would take my provision, so I was at peace. And I knew that no one else would do my deeds for me, so I busied myself with them.

It also makes it easier to leave what's haram, forbidden. Your provision is written: it won't increase or decrease. So why earn it the haram way and go to the Fire for nothing? Earn it the halal way, and it's the exact same provision.

And when prayer time comes while you're at work or studying, and Satan whispers: you'll lose a customer, remember that the One who commanded you to pray is the same One who wrote your provision, and His command can never conflict with His provision. Allah says:

وَأْمُرْ أَهْلَكَ بِالصَّلَاةِ وَاصْطَبِرْ عَلَيْهَا لَا نَسْأَلُكَ رِزْقًا نَحْنُ نَرْزُقُكَ

And command your family to pray, and be patient in it. We do not ask you for provision; We provide for you.

Second, knowing your lifespan is written makes the calamity of death easier to bear. When you know everyone's lifetime is written, you stop saying: if only he had done this, he wouldn't have died. Because Allah says:

قُلْ لَوْ كُنْتُمْ فِي بُيُوتِكُمْ لَبَرَزَ الَّذِينَ كُتِبَ عَلَيْهِمُ الْقَتْلُ إِلَىٰ مَضَاجِعِهِمْ

Say: Even if you had stayed in your homes, those for whom death in battle was decreed would have gone out to the places where they were to lie dead.

It also makes you brave in standing for the truth. Courage won't bring your death closer, and cowardice won't delay it. Khalid ibn al-Walid fought in many battles, wishing to die as a martyr, and in the end he died in his bed.

Third, knowing your deeds are written: if you do something good, don't think you're amazing and take the credit for your success! Allah says, meaning: nothing of the matter is in your hands. It was written for you before you were born, so it's a blessing from Allah upon you. And Allah says:

لِكَيْلَا تَأْسَوْا عَلَىٰ مَا فَاتَكُمْ وَلَا تَفْرَحُوا بِمَا آتَاكُمْ وَاللَّهُ لَا يُحِبُّ كُلَّ مُخْتَالٍ فَخُورٍ

So that you do not grieve over what has escaped you, nor exult over what He has given you. And Allah does not love anyone arrogant and boastful.

Fourth: wretched or happy. The companions asked: so why should we work? And the Prophet, peace be upon him, said: Act, for everyone will be eased toward what he was created for. So Allah didn't only write the result; He wrote the result and the means to it. As long as you're walking the path of good, that's good news.

Here's another example from the lesson: your provision is written, and yet you still go to work every day, right? No one sits at home saying: whatever is written will come. So why do we say it only about good deeds? A poor person works hard because maybe it's written that he'll become rich. So you work hard too, because maybe it's written that you'll be among the happy ones.

The Prophet, peace be upon him, said that when Allah creates a servant for Paradise, He makes him do the deeds of the people of Paradise until he dies upon them. This hadith is in the Musnad of Imam Ahmad, and al-Albani graded it authentic. So being guided to obedience is a good sign. Ask Allah to keep you firm.

Kinds of writing

So how many kinds of writing are there? There's the writing in al-Lawh al-Mahfuz, the Preserved Tablet, and that never changes. There's the writing for the baby in its mother's womb. And there's the yearly writing on Laylat al-Qadr, the Night of Decree. The writings with the angels can change, like a lifespan that grows longer through keeping family ties.

And all of this is already written in the Preserved Tablet, before the change and after it, because it's Allah's knowledge. That's why Allah says:

يَمْحُو اللَّهُ مَا يَشَاءُ وَيُثْبِتُ وَعِنْدَهُ أُمُّ الْكِتَابِ

Allah erases what He wills, and confirms what He wills, and with Him is the Mother of the Book.

The last arm's length

فَوَاَللَّهِ الَّذِي لَا إلَهَ غَيْرُهُ إنَّ أَحَدَكُمْ لَيَعْمَلُ بِعَمَلِ أَهْلِ الْجَنَّةِ حَتَّى مَا يَكُونُ بَيْنَهُ وَبَيْنَهَا إلَّا ذِرَاعٌ فَيَسْبِقُ عَلَيْهِ الْكِتَابُ فَيَعْمَلُ بِعَمَلِ أَهْلِ النَّارِ فَيَدْخُلُهَا.

By Allah, other than whom there is no god: one of you may do the deeds of the people of Paradise until there is only an arm's length between him and it. Then what has been written overtakes him, and he does the deeds of the people of the Fire, and so he enters it.

وَإِنَّ أَحَدَكُمْ لَيَعْمَلُ بِعَمَلِ أَهْلِ النَّارِ حَتَّى مَا يَكُونُ بَيْنَهُ وَبَيْنَهَا إلَّا ذِرَاعٌ فَيَسْبِقُ عَلَيْهِ الْكِتَابُ فَيَعْمَلُ بِعَمَلِ أَهْلِ الْجَنَّةِ فَيَدْخُلُهَا

And one of you may do the deeds of the people of the Fire until there is only an arm's length between him and it. Then what has been written overtakes him, and he does the deeds of the people of Paradise, and so he enters it.

The rule is: whoever lives on something usually dies on it. And the end of this hadith is a rare exception, so that no one feels too safe, and no one loses hope. The first person, Allah treated with justice. The second, Allah treated with grace.

So was the one with the bad ending a hypocrite? Apparently not: the hadith called his deeds “the deeds of the people of Paradise”, and he was as close to it as an arm's length. So what happened? Scholars mention some possibilities.

The first possibility: a hidden disease in the heart, like Iblis's arrogance, that was never treated until it showed up at the last moment. The second: sins done in private, which are the root of falling back. And the third: a small innovation in religion, a bid'ah, that grows until it overcomes the Sunnah.

An example from the lesson: a young man becomes religious and starts doing acts of worship seriously, but inside him there's an old attachment, say, to the love of fame, and he leaves it untreated. When the test comes, money and fame against his religion, the disease suddenly shows. That's why we must treat our hearts as we go.

This makes us fear a bad ending, and say often the Prophet's prayer, peace be upon him: O Turner of hearts, make my heart firm upon Your religion. And if someone is far from Allah, this hadith gives them hope: they can come back, even at the very last moment.

And a final benefit: we don't judge anyone as going to Paradise or Hell. We don't say: this person will never enter Paradise, or: that person is surely one of the people of Paradise. We deal with what we see, and the ending is known only to Allah.

Review & practice

Let's review: the hadith describes the stages of creation, then the writing of provision, lifespan, deeds, and wretched or happy. This writing gives us peace, courage and humility, and it pushes us to work, not to be lazy. And we ask Allah for a good ending.

Your practice this week: when you worry about your provision, remember it's written, and choose halal. Check your heart for any hidden disease. And repeat every day: O Turner of hearts, make my heart firm upon Your religion.
